# Company Brain - index

The map of this vault. Read this first.
Pattern: raw input in `sources/` -> compiled pages in `wiki/` -> read on demand.
See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) for the routing table, the closed allowlist, and the rules.

This brain is empty to start. Add pages as you go and keep this map current.

## sources/ (immutable raw material)

- `meetings/` - transcripts and call notes, one file per meeting (`YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`).
- `research/` - external captures: articles, scrapes, transcripts.
- `notes/` - fleeting notes and insights (`YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`).

## wiki/ (the compiled brain)

- `sop/` - standard operating procedures the team follows. Start: [[welcome]].
- `brands/` - compact per-brand or per-product checklists.
- `people/` - people we work with.
- `clients/` - client and account overviews.
- `tools/` - tool and system references.
- `concepts/` - concepts and frameworks.
- `decisions/` - decisions and their reasoning.
- `meetings/` - distilled meeting summaries, one per meeting.
- `_templates/` - blank page templates (`wiki-page`, `source`).

## archive/ (finished or superseded)

Free-form. Nothing here is current.
