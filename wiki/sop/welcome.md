## What this is

This is your company brain: one place for the SOPs, checklists, and reference pages the whole team relies on.
It is written in plain Markdown, kept in git, and published to this read site automatically.
If something about how the team works lives only in someone's head or a chat thread, it belongs here.

## How it stays current

A small core writes and edits the pages; everyone else reads them.
Only pages marked for the team appear on this site, so the source vault can hold internal notes without exposing them here.
When a process changes, the page changes - there is no separate "update the docs" step to forget.

## How to add to it

- Writing a page? Follow the house rules in the vault's `AGENTS.md`: one concept per page, cross-link related pages, no secrets.
- Not sure where something goes? The routing table in `AGENTS.md` has a row for it.
- Found something out of date? Fix the page and note what changed.
